Part of [The python tests](../TESTING_PYTHON.md): the groups of suite 5.2 that test the front page, the site's other pages, the hub, nginx, the web build without Docker and the game server's scripts.

- **The pace of the front page's requests:** every kind (a color move, a plan, a rename, a removal, a line of chat) keeps a burst one smaller and 0.8 of the rate of the server's bucket of its kind (`BUDGETS` in `web/front/lobby_net.js`, the server's in `include/ants_net/flood.hpp`): the server counts the moment at which it HANDLES a request and drops an early one without a word. `web_lobby_client_check.js` reads the server's limits from `flood.hpp` and replays what a page asks for (at every millisecond for 16 s, in three runs, after 5 s of quiet) through the server's own bucket with every combination of 0 and 50, 100 or 150 ms of delay for the first 14 requests, 300 random delays over all of them, and one request held up by 333 ms (a color move, a plan) or 1400 ms (the rest) with the others on time: none may be dropped, and the same replay drops requests that are at exactly the server's own rate. `web_lobby_server_check.js` (run by `test_ants_server.sh`) has pages that ask as fast as the client lets them on a link that jitters by up to 150 ms, a rename and a plan for 8.5 s and a color move for 6 s: every request that was sent is applied. `web_name_check.js` (the whole page in a fake browser) holds that a team press that the page's pace refuses shows the room's teams, not a pair that the room never got. The mutants of the pace (`tests/data/lobby_budget_mutants.json`: a pace moved back to the server's own rate or burst, a margin that is too small, a pace that is too slow, a budget that is not spent or is shared with another kind, a team press that the pace refused and the screen keeps) are run by `test_web_lobby_net.py` on a copy of the files and by `tools/mutate.py tests/data/lobby_budget_mutants.json --keep-going --jobs 1`; all must be caught.
